# Privacy policy — Camera HT / Field Camera

Updated: 3 November 2026. Applies to update 1.1.0 for Android and iOS, application identifier com.datvip.cam. Earlier DatVip Cam versions 1.0.1–1.0.2 contain no advertising or in-app purchases; their policy remains on the home page.

## Camera, location and your content

Camera HT / Field Camera captures photos and videos with stamps for time, location, coordinates, tasks, notes and logos. Photos, videos, audio, logos and custom templates are processed and stored on your device. The developer has no server receiving or storing this content and the app requires no app account.

The camera provides preview and capture. The microphone is used for video audio only with permission. Precise location, altitude and heading sensors support stamps and map links when you grant permission. Background location permission is not requested. Android stops location updates when the app screen pauses; on iOS location is used while the app is in use.

Address lookup uses the geocoding service supplied by Android or iOS. Coordinates may be sent over a network to that service provider under its policies. Hiding an address or coordinate stamp does not disable location or address lookup; revoke location permission in system settings to stop new location access.

When you choose to open a map or share content or a link, the selected app or recipient receives that content. Stamps and photo metadata may contain precise location and work information. Check content before sharing.

## Advertising and privacy choices in version 1.1.0

The update integrates Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP) for a small banner below the camera controls and privacy choices where required. Banners are not included in saved photos or videos. The app does not explicitly attach photos, videos, notes, logos or GPS coordinates to ad requests.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses and coarse location derived from IP or system permissions, permitted device/app/advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google's handling and retention are subject to its policies and applicable choices and permissions. The developer cannot remotely delete data held by Google.

The app checks UMP privacy status before requesting ads. Where required, an applicable privacy form is presented and an advertising privacy options entry lets you revisit your choices. Declining personalization may still allow limited ads depending on your choices and applicable requirements. System settings also let you manage advertising identifiers and permissions.

## One-time ad removal purchase

Google Play Billing on Android and Apple StoreKit on iOS handle purchases. The store displays the actual localized price before confirmation. The developer receives no card or bank account details. The app verifies transaction status, product identifiers and transaction evidence to determine ad removal entitlement. Android stores the receipt and signature in private app storage. Pending payments do not unlock ad removal.

Once entitlement is verified, the app hides the banner and stops making new ad requests. You can restore purchases using the store account that bought the product. Google Play and App Store purchases are managed separately. Clearing app data does not issue a refund or delete store purchase history; use the store's support tools for these requests.

## Storage, deletion and security

Language, stamp settings, templates and logos are stored in app storage. Exports are saved in the device photo library; Android retains existing DatvipCam folders for compatibility. Delete media in the library, delete templates, replace logos, clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app content. Uninstalling may leave exported media, backups, shared copies and store purchase history. Delete these separately with the relevant service or recipient.

The operating system and photo services may back up data under your settings. The app has no proprietary synchronization server. Private data is protected by operating system app isolation; exported content is subject to device library access permissions. Geocoding, mapping and sharing services have their own data handling and retention policies outside the developer's control.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your address and the content you send to respond to your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence through the contact address, subject to identity verification and legal retention requirements. The developer cannot remotely access or delete content held only on your device.

## Children, changes and contact

The app is intended for recording work and is not directed at children. You can grant or revoke camera, microphone, location and photo permissions in system settings; related features may be unavailable without permission. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
